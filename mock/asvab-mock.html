Percent problems: part = % × whole

Every percent question hides three numbers: the part, the percent and the whole. You are always given two and asked for the third. Write the three slots before touching the numbers.

Worked example — A jacket costs $80 and is marked 25 % off. What is the sale price? Slots: whole = 80, percent = 25, part = ? → part = 0.25 × 80 = 20. The question asks for the sale price, not the discount: 80 − 20 = $60.

Trap to name: the intermediate value. $20 will be one of the four choices. Reread the last sentence of the question before you click.

How the test is beaten

Six rules of the adaptive test

    Test day

    Photo ID. No calculator, ever. Arrive early — late means rescheduled. Sleep instead of cramming, eat breakfast, use the bathroom before the test. Do not press stray keys. Retest waits: 1 month, 1 month, then 6 months, and the most recent score counts, not the highest.
