# ASVAB Strategies, Clues and Tricks — Full Detail

Raw material for the app's "Strategy" section and for the hint/explanation text on each question. Sources listed at the end.

## A. General CAT-ASVAB strategy

### How the adaptive engine works
- Every subtest begins with a medium-difficulty item. Correct → harder next item; wrong → easier. Scores are estimated from the difficulty of the items you get right, not the raw count.
- You cannot go back, skip, or change an answer once submitted. Verify before you click Next.
- The first ~5 items of each subtest steer the difficulty trajectory; spend 3–4 min on early AR items and ~90 s on early MK items if needed.
- Strings of consecutive wrong answers drag the estimate down fast. If unsure, eliminate, guess, move on.
- Hard questions are a good sign: the algorithm converges everyone to ~50% correct.
- A single wrong guess carries no penalty. Running out of time, or random-guessing a series of items at the end, is penalized ("relatively large penalty" per the official site). So pace to finish with legitimate attempts.
- Reading instructions doesn't consume test time; a HELP key pauses the timer if you need the proctor.

### Universal elimination rules
1. Drop absolute answers ("always", "never", "entirely") in verbal/science items.
2. When two choices are exact opposites, one of them is usually correct.
3. When two choices mean the same thing, both are wrong.
4. If you cannot eliminate anything, guess the same letter every time.
5. Never infer a pattern from previous answers.
6. If stuck for 1–2 minutes, guess and move on. Use scratch paper freely.

### Test day
Government photo ID; no calculator (ever); arrive early (late = rescheduled); sleep instead of cramming; eat; dress comfortably; don't talk or press stray keys (can invalidate). Test items are controlled material — don't share them afterward.

### Retest and PiCAT rules
- Retest: 1 calendar month → 1 calendar month → 6 months. Most recent score counts, not highest. +20 AFQT in 6 months → Confirmation Test; missing it costs 6 months. Scores valid 2 years.
- PiCAT: first-time takers only, once; code valid 30 days; 48 h from start; 2–3 h of work; no help, no calculator, screen ≥ 7". Verification Test at MEPS within 45 days: 25–30 min consistency check, no score shown; if not verified → full ASVAB the same day becomes score of record. Don't start the PiCAT until two recent timed practice tests land within ~5 AFQT points of each other; keep 15 min/day maintenance between PiCAT and Vtest.

## B. Per-subtest strategies

### General Science (GS)
- Yield order: life science (cells, body systems, genetics, ecology, classification) > physical science (atoms, periodic table, states of matter, pH, Newton's laws, energy/heat) > earth/space (Earth layers, plate tectonics, rock cycle, weather, solar system).
- One flashcard per fact, not per topic — GS rewards ~300 small facts.
- Mnemonics: King Philip Came Over For Good Soup (Kingdom→Species); My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Nachos (planets).
- Fact drill: 206 bones; 4 heart chambers; red blood cells carry O₂; photosynthesis CO₂+H₂O→glucose+O₂; pH 7 neutral, <7 acid; Newton's three laws; light faster than sound; SI units m, kg, s, N, J, W; water boils 100 °C / 212 °F, freezes 0 °C / 32 °F.
- Trap: units. "Water boils at 212 °C" is false. Read the unit before the number.

### Arithmetic Reasoning (AR) — AFQT
- WANT / HAVE / CONNECT on every word problem: what is asked, what is given, which formula bridges them.
- Five formulas ≈ 80% of items: d = r·t; part = % × whole; cross-multiplication for ratios; area; average = sum ÷ count.
- Backsolve: plug choices in starting with B or C, then go up or down.
- Signal words: "5 less than x" = x − 5; "of" = ×; "per" = ÷; "more than" = +; "how much more" = subtract; "how many times" = divide.
- Mental math: 4 × 53 = 200 + 12; ÷4 = halve twice; 15% of 80 = 10% + 5% = 8 + 4; estimate first to kill wrong choices; memorize 1/8 = .125, 1/4 = .25, 1/3 ≈ .333, 3/8 = .375, 5/8 = .625, 3/4 = .75.
- Traps: answering an intermediate value instead of what was asked (remainder vs total); unit mismatch (min vs h, cents vs dollars).
- You have 3+ min per item: read twice, write the setup, check the answer against the question.

### Word Knowledge (WK) — AFQT, highest leverage
- Two formats: "X most nearly means…" and an underlined word in a sentence.
- Each WK raw point ≈ 1.31 AFQT points — cheapest points on the test.
- Tricks: decompose into prefix/root/suffix; say it aloud (cognates); guess polarity from the prefix and eliminate opposite-polarity choices; substitute each choice into the sentence.
- Prefixes: a-/an- not · ab- away · anti-/contra-/counter- against · bene- good · mal- bad · bi- two · circum- around · com-/con- with · de- away from · dis- not/reverse · extra- beyond · fore-/pre- before · post- after · hyper-/super-/over- above · hypo-/sub- under · il-/un-/non- not · inter- between · mis- wrong · micro- small · multi- many · omni- all · re- again · semi- half · tele- far · trans- across.
- Suffixes: -able/-ible capable of · -ful full of · -less without · -ous full of · -ist one who · -er/-or one who does · -ify make · -ion/-tion/-sion act/process · -ity/-ness state · -ive tending to · -ism belief · -ish resembling · -let small · -ology study of.
- Roots: spect look · graph/scrib write · bio life · vent come · clude close · potent power · onym name · mut change · ten hold · volent wish · dict say · port carry · chron time · cred believe · vid/vis see.
- Study: 10 new words/day, 15 min/day → 200+ words in 4 weeks.

### Paragraph Comprehension (PC) — AFQT
- Four types: main idea, detail, inference, vocabulary-in-context.
- Method: read the question stem first → read the passage for that task → predict → match.
- Main idea unclear? Reread first and last sentence.
- Traps: too-specific / too-general main-idea choices; details from an irrelevant sentence; inference choices that add outside knowledge or use extremes; negative stems ("NOT", "EXCEPT").
- Inference check: "Would the author agree, based only on what is written?" Vocab-in-context: the choice must be able to replace the word in the sentence.
- ~2.5 min per item — reread when unsure.

### Mathematics Knowledge (MK) — AFQT
- ~55% algebra, ~45% geometry; no formula sheet.
- Memorize: areas (rectangle lw, triangle ½bh, circle πr², trapezoid ½(b₁+b₂)h); circumference 2πr; volumes (box lwh, cylinder πr²h); a²+b²=c² with 3-4-5 and 5-12-13; triangle 180°, quadrilateral 360°; slope (y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁); y = mx + b; quadratic formula; exponent rules (xᵃ·xᵇ = xᵃ⁺ᵇ, (xᵃ)ᵇ = xᵃᵇ, x⁰ = 1, x⁻ⁿ = 1/xⁿ); squares to 15²; FOIL; x²−y² = (x+y)(x−y). Write the list from memory daily.
- Pick numbers when answers contain variables (use 2, 3, 10; avoid −1, 0, 1). Backsolve quadratics from B/C.
- Traps: distributing negatives; PEMDAS; both roots of x² = 9; area vs perimeter; radius vs diameter; inequality flips when × or ÷ by a negative.
- ~2 min per item — half of AR. Drill formulas to automaticity.

### Electronics Information (EI)
- Ohm's law V = IR; power P = VI = I²R = V²/R. Units: volt (pressure), amp (current), ohm (resistance), watt (power), watt-hour (energy).
- Series: one path; same current; R_total = ΣR; voltages add; one break kills all. Parallel: same voltage on every branch; currents add; 1/R_eq = Σ1/R (two equal → R/2; R_eq < smallest branch); one break leaves others on.
- R = ρL/A: longer → more resistance, thicker → less. Copper best common conductor; rubber/glass/wood insulators.
- Components: diode one-way valve (rectifies AC→DC); resistor opposes both ways; capacitor stores charge, passes AC/blocks DC; inductor opposes current change; transformer steps AC voltage; transistor amplifies/switches (emitter, base, collector); fuse/breaker opens on overcurrent.
- Facts: US household 120 V / 60 Hz AC; DC one-way (batteries); AWG smaller number = thicker wire; resistor color code Black Brown Red Orange Yellow Green Blue Violet Gray White (0–9), gold ±5%, silver ±10%; ground green/bare, neutral white, hot black; schematic symbols for battery, resistor, capacitor, diode, switch, ground, lamp.
- Traps: multiplying instead of dividing in Ohm's law; mixing series/parallel rules. 40 s per item — recognize or move.

### Auto & Shop Information (AI + SI)
- Four-stroke: Intake → Compression → Power → Exhaust ("suck-squeeze-bang-blow"). Diesel ignites by compression, no spark plugs. Stoichiometric gasoline 14.7:1. Inline-4 firing order often 1-3-4-2.
- Systems: cooling (water pump, thermostat, radiator); lubrication (viscosity); ignition timing (before/after TDC); electrical (12 V battery, alternator charges, starter cranks); brakes (hydraulic; disc vs drum); drivetrain (clutch, transmission, driveshaft, differential); exhaust (catalytic converter, muffler).
- Tools: box-end vs open-end vs socket wrench; vise-grips; pipe wrench; auger bit; chisel; files; calipers and micrometer (precision); hacksaw (metal), crosscut (across grain), rip (with grain), coping (curves); plane; punch/awl; level/square.
- Fasteners/joining: bolt/nut/washer; wood vs machine screw; rivet; welding (melts base metal), brazing/soldering (filler only, solder lowest temp); wood joints (butt, lap, miter, dovetail, mortise-and-tenon).
- Trick: infer the tool's use from its shape; eliminate tools from the wrong domain.

### Mechanical Comprehension (MC)
- MA = load ÷ effort = effort distance ÷ load distance; F = ma; W = F·d; P = W/t; torque = F·r; pressure = F/A; Pascal F₁/A₁ = F₂/A₂; momentum mv; KE ½mv²; PE mgh; density m/V; compound MA = product.
- Levers: F₁d₁ = F₂d₂. Class 1 fulcrum middle (seesaw); Class 2 load middle (wheelbarrow, MA > 1); Class 3 effort middle (tweezers, MA < 1).
- Pulleys: MA = supporting rope segments; fixed pulley MA 1 (direction only).
- Gears: ratio = driven teeth ÷ driver teeth; small driving big → more torque, less speed; meshed gears reverse; same shaft/belt same direction; odd number of gears in a train → last turns like the first.
- Inclined plane MA = length ÷ height; screw MA = 2πr ÷ pitch; wheel-and-axle MA = wheel r ÷ axle r.
- Traps: "less effort ≠ less work"; fixed pulley MA 1; direction reversal in gear trains; hydraulics: small piston moves farther; lower center of gravity = more stable; narrower pipe = faster flow. Sketch forces.

### Assembling Objects (AO)
- Two item types: connectors (shapes joined at labeled points) and puzzles (pieces reassembled).
- Rotation allowed, mirror never — any flipped shape eliminates the choice.
- Connectors: check exactly where the dot sits (point vs indentation, corner vs side) and where the line enters.
- Puzzles: pick the most distinctive piece and scan all four choices for it; eliminate choices with an extra, missing, or resized piece; verify with a second piece.
- Practice with tangrams, jigsaws, sketching objects from other angles.

## C. Study plan logic

- AFQT = 2·VE + AR + MK; VE ≈ 0.655·WK + 0.406·PC − 3.03. WK ≈ 1.31 AFQT pts/point, PC ≈ 0.81, AR/MK 1.0.
- Tiers: 1) WK + PC · 2) AR · 3) MK · 4) technical subtests required by the target job (confirm with recruiter).
- Diagnostic Day 1 → % correct per subtest → AFQT bucket vs composite bucket → plan by gap: ≤5 pts → 2 weeks; 10–15 → 4 weeks; 15+ → 8 weeks.
- 2-week (45–60 min/day): diagnostic; drill two weakest AFQT subtests; full timed tests Day 7 and 13; last 48 h light review.
- 4-week (30–45 min/day, 6 days/week): Wk1 diagnostic + weakest daily; Wk2 add second weakest; Wk3 timed drills across all AFQT subtests; Wk4 mixed review + technical. Weekly full test.
- 8-week: Wks 1–4 untimed fundamentals; Wks 5–6 timed drills (score jump shows here); Wks 7–8 full tests, error review, technical.
- Session shape: 10 min flashcards → 50–60 min new material → 10–15 min recall. Spaced repetition 1 d → 3 d → 1 wk → 1 mo, from the student's own missed items. Three 30-min sessions beat one 90-min block. 60% weak / 40% strong. Keep timed vs untimed accuracy within 10 points. Ready when errors stop repeating, timed sets finish without rushing, and 2–3 practice scores are stable.
